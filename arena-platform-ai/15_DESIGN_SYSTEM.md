# Design System

Brand: ArenaFlow

Tagline: Run tournaments. Track every point.

## Visual direction
- modern
- athletic
- professional
- clean
- high readability
- restrained motion

## Suggested palette
Primary: deep green
Surface: white/off-white
Text: charcoal
Success: green
Warning: amber
Danger: red
Live: high-attention accent

Do not hard-code colors across components; use design tokens.

## Typography
Use a clear sans-serif family with strong numerical readability. Scores should use tabular/monospaced numerals where available.

## Motion
Use motion to communicate state, not decoration. Scoring must remain fast and accessible.
